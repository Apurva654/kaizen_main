Chess Rules & Guide

Objective

Checkmate the opponent's king. Your pieces trap the king so it cannot escape capture.

Special Moves

  • Castling: Move the king two squares toward a rook to castle (if neither has moved and path is clear).
  • En Passant: Capture an enemy pawn that has just moved two squares forward, as if it moved only one.
  • Promotion: When a pawn reaches the opposite end, promote it to a Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight.

AI Difficulty

Easy makes random legal moves. Medium evaluates simple captures and checks. Hard uses advanced minimax with material and positional evaluation.
